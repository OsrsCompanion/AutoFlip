What is AutoFlip?
AutoFlip is an OSRS-first trading co-pilot. It helps players understand market opportunities, compare flips, and make smarter Grand Exchange decisions with less manual research.
Is AutoFlip beginner friendly?
Yes. AutoFlip is designed for players who want GP but do not want to spend hours learning every detail of flipping. The goal is simple guidance, clear reasons, and less guessing.
Does AutoFlip click or type for me?
No. AutoFlip is guided, not automated. You stay in control and place every offer yourself. The product does not auto-click, auto-type, bot, or play the game for you.
Do I need the RuneLite plugin?
The website helps with discovery, trust, and market exploration. The plugin is the future execution surface for faster board guidance, slot runbooks, and player-aware planning.
Where does AutoFlip get its market data?
AutoFlip uses the OSRS Wiki real-time pricing ecosystem as its primary public market source, then builds cache-first views for faster website and plugin use.
Why are some flips marked low confidence?
A flip can look profitable but still be risky if the margin is stale, the volume is weak, the spread is unstable, or the quantity is unrealistic for your time window.
Is AutoFlip allowed?
AutoFlip is designed as a guidance tool. It does not automate gameplay. Users remain responsible for following Jagex and RuneLite rules.
What is the difference between free and premium?
The free website focuses on market discovery and partial value. Premium intelligence is planned for deeper board guidance, stronger confidence scoring, alerts, and plugin-powered workflow features.
